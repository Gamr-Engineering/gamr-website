# **Build Africa on Roblox — Summer Edition**

**In partnership with LSETF**

# **Hero / Intro**

This summer, we’re building the future of African gaming on Roblox.

Build Africa on Roblox — Summer Edition is a focused creator development program designed to help young African builders, developers, designers, and storytellers level up their Roblox skills, work on real projects, and plug into a growing ecosystem of opportunities across games, experiences, and virtual worlds.

Delivered by Gamr Studios and Gamr Lab in partnership with LSETF, this program is part of a wider mission to equip African youth with practical digital skills, creative confidence, and pathways into the global gaming and creator economy.

Following the success and learnings from our first Roblox cohort, we are taking the next step: a sharper, more execution-driven summer program focused on learning, building, collaboration, and shipping real Roblox experiences.

# **What this program is about**

Build Africa on Roblox — Summer Edition is designed to help African creators move from interest to execution.

Through structured learning, mentorship, and project-based work, participants will develop the skills needed to build better Roblox experiences and understand how games, worlds, and digital IP can create real opportunities.

* Helping African Roblox creators build higher-quality, more ambitious experiences  
* Introducing participants to real game development workflows and studio-style thinking  
* Supporting young creators with practical skills in Roblox Studio, world-building, scripting, design, UI/UX, and AI-enhanced creation  
* Connecting participants with a serious community of builders, mentors, and collaborators  
* Preparing talent for future opportunities in Roblox development, monetisation, IP creation, and studio pipelines

This is not just a class. It is a creator pipeline for young African talent ready to build culturally relevant, globally competitive Roblox experiences.

# **Why LSETF and Gamr are doing this**

Africa has one of the youngest and most creative populations in the world, but many young people still need access to the tools, structure, mentorship, and opportunities required to turn their creativity into real digital careers.

Through this partnership, LSETF and Gamr are creating a practical pathway for young people to learn future-facing skills in game development, digital creation, collaboration, and entrepreneurship.

Roblox is more than a gaming platform. It is a global creation ecosystem where young developers can build worlds, tell stories, create experiences, form teams, and earn from their work.

Build Africa on Roblox — Summer Edition is designed to help more African creators participate meaningfully in that ecosystem.

# **Who this is for**

This program is for you if:

* You already build, experiment, or create on Roblox  
* You are interested in scripting, game design, world-building, UI/UX, 3D environments, systems, or storytelling  
* You are serious about improving your craft and working with other committed creators  
* You are based in Lagos / Nigeria or part of the African gaming and creator ecosystem  
* You want to learn how to take Roblox creation more seriously — from hobby to skill, portfolio, project, or career pathway

You do not have to be a professional developer yet.

But you do need to show intent. That could be through a Roblox profile, past project, portfolio, prototype, design work, or anything that proves you are genuinely interested in building.

# **What you can expect**

Selected participants will join a structured summer program built around learning, practice, and execution.

You can expect:

* Practical sessions on Roblox Studio, world-building, scripting, design, and experience creation  
* Clear weekly milestones, sprints, and deliverables  
* Mentorship and feedback from Gamr’s creator and studio network  
* Exposure to how a Roblox-focused studio thinks, builds, and ships  
* Collaboration with other African Roblox builders  
* Opportunities to showcase your work  
* A pathway into future Gamr Lab, Gamr Studios, partner, and creator economy opportunities

The goal is simple: help participants become stronger builders and prepare them to create Roblox experiences that can be showcased, published, improved, and potentially monetised.

# **What you will build**

Participants will work toward creating Roblox experiences inspired by African creativity, culture, cities, communities, stories, and futures.

Possible project directions include:

* African-inspired virtual worlds  
* Lagos-themed environments  
* Social and multiplayer experiences  
* Educational or cultural Roblox experiences  
* Mini-games and interactive experiences  
* AI-enhanced NPCs or gameplay ideas  
* UI/UX-driven Roblox prototypes

By the end of the program, participants should have stronger skills, clearer portfolios, and real project work that demonstrates their growth.

Apply for Build Africa on Roblox — Summer Edition, delivered by Gamr Studios and Gamr Lab in partnership with LSETF.
