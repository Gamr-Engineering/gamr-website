  
**Build Africa on Roblox — Summer Edition (Cohort 2\)**

**Go-To-Market & Launch Plan**  
**Gamr Studios / Gamr Lab**

---

1. Initiative Overview

---

Initiative Name  
 Build Africa on Roblox — Summer Edition (Cohort 2\)

Description  
This initiative launches the second cohort of the “Build Africa on Roblox” program, focused on a Summer Edition that deepens Gamr’s presence on Roblox, re-engages alumni, attracts new African Roblox creators, and strengthens Gamr Lab as a year-round Roblox creation hub.

The Summer Edition should:

* Reinforce Gamr Lab as the go-to Roblox creation and production hub in Africa.

* Activate and expand the alumni and wider gaming community.

* Grow in-house Roblox development capacity and repeatable production pipelines under the Gamr Studios brand.

* Lay further groundwork for scalable IP, brand partnerships, and monetisation opportunities.

---

2. Core Objective

---

Primary Objective  
 Launch a structured and coordinated call for Roblox developers for the Summer Edition that:

* Positions Gamr Lab as the home of African Roblox creators (with clear Summer Edition branding and narrative).

* Re-activates Cohort 1 alumni and pulls in new high-signal Roblox talent.

* Strengthens internal Roblox production capacity and processes.

* Generates measurable momentum for future cohorts, IP development, and partnerships.

Short-Term Success Criteria

* Public Summer Edition launch goes live on 17th Friday July 2026

* Developer applications begin within 48 hours of launch.

* All internal workstreams are owned and executed with clear accountability.

* Application review and shortlisting process is defined and operational by launch week.

---

3. Execution Timeline

---

Execution Window  
13th July –17th July

This is a focused 5-day GTM sprint optimised for speed, clarity, and ownership. The goal is to execute like a studio launch.

5. Operating Principles

---

* All clarifications and blockers are handled asynchronously in the agreed channel.

* Each workstream owner is fully responsible for delivery and communication.

* Bias for speed and clarity over complexity; ship first, optimise after.

* Treat this like a studio product launch, not a one-off announcement.

---

6. Launch Day Checklist

---

Before Launch Day

* Application form is live, tested, and connected to tracking sheet.

* All visual assets are finalised and accessible to social and influencer leads.

* Website Summer Edition section is live, QA’d, and mobile-friendly.

* Paid promotion setup is complete and scheduled (if using Day 1 ads).

* Email and social copy approved and scheduled.

On Launch Day

* Social media launch posts and Newsletter go live as planned.

* Alumni outreach message is sent.

* Influencer(s)/community partners post or confirm posting window.

* Applications monitored in real time for:

  * Early volume and quality.

  * Any technical issues (broken links, form issues, tracking problems).

* Quick internal check-in at end of day to review early signal.

8. Post-Launch Next Steps (Preview)

---

* Week 1: Review application volume, quality, and channel performance; adjust promotion if the application window is still open.

* Shortlist and contact candidates for interviews/onboarding based on predefined criteria.

* Run an internal retro on the 5-day GTM sprint: what worked, what broke, what we standardise for Cohort 3\.

* Begin planning for Roblox skill onboarding, team formation, and production pipeline for the Summer Edition (clear milestones, sprints, and ownership).

* Capture testimonials, behind-the-scenes content, and learnings from Cohort 1 and the new Summer Edition to build long-term storytelling and credibility around “Build Africa on Roblox.”
